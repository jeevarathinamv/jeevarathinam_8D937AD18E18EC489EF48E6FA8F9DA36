Entrepreneurship Resources

Beginner-friendly guides and tools to help MCA and college students convert ideas into successful startups.

1. Business Idea Development

Learn how to identify real-world problems and convert them into feasible technology and business concepts.

  • Define the core problem clearly.
  • Brainstorm multiple solution avenues.
  • Validate early hypotheses with real users.
  • Focus on unique value propositions.
2. Market Research

Understand your target audience, analyze industry competitors, and estimate addressable market size.

  • Conduct surveys and customer interviews.
  • Identify key market competitors and gaps.
  • Define Total Addressable Market (TAM).
  • Analyze customer buying behavior.
3. Business Plan Creation

Structure your executive summary, revenue model, operational plan, and growth strategies concisely.

  • Formulate the Business Model Canvas (BMC).
  • Detail key operational activities & partners.
  • Outline clear revenue streams.
  • Plan short-term and long-term milestones.
4. Marketing Basics

Master essential branding, digital marketing, social media promotion, and customer acquisition tactics.

  • Build an authentic brand story.
  • Leverage social media and content marketing.
  • Utilize cost-effective campus marketing.
  • Focus on customer retention and referrals.
5. Budget Planning

Estimate startup costs, manage working capital, and plan financial projections accurately.

  • Separate fixed costs vs variable costs.
  • Calculate the Break-Even Point (BEP).
  • Maintain lean initial operational expenses.
  • Seek college grants and seed funds.
6. Startup Tips

Practical advice on team building, seeking faculty mentorship, pitching to investors, and networking.

  • Build a balanced co-founding team.
  • Get feedback from industry mentors.
  • Practice 2-minute elevator pitches.
  • Participate in college hackathons & expos.
