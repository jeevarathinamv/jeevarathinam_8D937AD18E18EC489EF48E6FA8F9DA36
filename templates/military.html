Kandalur Salai (988 CE)

Celebrated as Kandalur Salai Kalamarutha in royal inscriptions. Raja Raja's armada routed the Chera naval fleet at Vizhinjam harbor, dismantling Western coastal resistance.

Invasion

Mummudi Chola Mandalam (993 CE)

Chola naval forces crossed Palk Strait to capture Anuradhapura, establishing Polonnaruwa (renamed Jananathamangalam) as the provincial capital and building the Shiva Devale.

Polonnaruwa Garrison
Deccan Conquest

Western Chalukya Frontiers

Decisive land expeditions annexed Nolambapadi, Gangavadi, and Tadigaipadi (modern Karnataka), setting up fortified border posts against Chalukya King Satyashraya.

Munnir Pazhantivu (1014 CE)

His final victorious naval campaign incorporated the Maldives archipelago ("twelve thousand ancient islands of the sea"), cementing total control over Indian Ocean trade routes.
