Key System Features

Designed for fast, simple, and transparent hostel administration.

View Current Room

Students can instantly view their assigned Hostel Block, Room Number, Room Type, and Allocation Date.

Request Room Change

Submit requests choosing requested rooms and reasons such as Roommate Issue, Maintenance, or Medical grounds with automated Unique Request IDs.

Admin Room Control

Admins manage room bed capacities, review requests, inspect room availability, and automatically update allocations upon approval.
