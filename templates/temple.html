UNESCO World Heritage Monument

Thanjavur Brihadeeswarar Temple

பெருவுடையார் கோயில் • தக்ஷிண மேரு (Daksina Meru)

Commissioned in 1003 CE and consecrated in 1010 CE, this granite marvel is the world's first complete granite temple, towering 216 feet into the Tamil sky.

13 Storey Vimana 80-Ton Monolithic Cupola 130,000+ Tons Granite

The 80-Ton Kumbam

The crowning monolithic granite cupola weighs roughly 80 metric tons. Engineers rolled it atop the 66-meter Vimana using an inclined earthen ramp extending over 6 kilometers from Sarapallam.

Zero-Mortar Interlocking

Built completely without binding cement or mortar. Massive granite blocks were precision cut and dry-fitted using jigsaw puzzle interlocking joints that have withstood 6 major earthquakes.

Stone Inscription Register

Raja Raja Cholan immortalized every worker on temple walls: chief architect Kunjaramallan Rajaraja Perunthachan, sculptors, 400 devadasis, musicians, and treasurers with exact wage allocations.

Frescoes & Chola Bronzes

The circumambulatory passage contains radiant 11th-century Chola fresco paintings depicting Lord Shiva as Tripurantaka and Raja Raja Cholan receiving blessings from Saint Karuvur Devar.
