Our Story & Heritage

Where Tradition Meets Contemporary Dining

Founded in 1998, Spice Garden began with a solitary vision: to curate an unforgettable sensory journey rooted in authenticity, warmth, and culinary vibrancy.

Our kitchens honour regional master recipes passed down through generations. From hand-pounded aromatic garam masalas and stone-ground coconut chutneys to charcoal-smoked clay tandoor dishes, every single meal is an ode to heartfelt culinary storytelling.
